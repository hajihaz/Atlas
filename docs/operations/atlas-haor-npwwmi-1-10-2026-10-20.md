## Reality of the cloud scheduler
Cron is `17 */2 * * *`, in UTC: nominally 00:17, 02:17, 04:17 ... UTC (05:47, 07:47, 09:47 ... IST). GitHub's schedule is **best effort**. The first paper runner was a controlled `push` event, NOT a successful `schedule` event. At the sprint start, it had one source-verified checkpoint, five hypothetical portfolios, and zero events. A second genuine cron event cannot be fabricated or brought forward. The UI reports the next nominal slot and labels an overdue/no-new-publication condition after a 45-minute delay; that condition does not prove job failure.
