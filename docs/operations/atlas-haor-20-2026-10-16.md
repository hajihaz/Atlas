is **not** a declaration that Atlas is production-trading ready. Neither

  live broker orders, real funds, unrelated Jarvis repository and production DBs.

  production, third-party or sustained-reliability certification.

## Owner and external gates

Real-market provider/token permission, real exchange data rights, managed
production PostgreSQL/backup/restore, production secrets/network isolation,
external alert transport, compliance/legal approval and independent human
capital authorization cannot be manufactured by unit tests. Atlas must remain
BLOCKED until every gate independently passes. An intermittent Python.NET
upstream runtime crash also prevents full LEAN NUnit reliability certification
despite its independently passing trading-engine golden fixture suite.
