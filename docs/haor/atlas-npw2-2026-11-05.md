## Persistence and scheduling

Additive HISTORY_DB tables store exact context-provider bytes separately from compact hashed decision inputs and raw calendar receipts. Existing paper accounts are not migrated or reset. Decision hashes and method versions bind new intents/fills to persisted journals. One failing additional strategy cannot suppress X's committed journal. Context reads are batched once per strategy rather than repeated per stock/window.

The existing four Cron families remain four: morning dated calendar and GIFT capture; one company-context slot per invocation; completed-minute decision journals; independent seven-account settlement. No extra ChatGPT automation, paid infrastructure, broker-order route or credential export is introduced.
